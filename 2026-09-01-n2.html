The world

CNN, Front Office Sports, D Magazine · 27-28 Aug, 1 Sep 2026

Good Good Golf, from a 'horror' spot to dropped sponsors

A spot showing co-founder Garrett Clark shoulder-checking Alexis Miestowski to the ground as she reaches for a Callaway co-branded driver is published and pulled shortly after. The CEO says the full 60-second video was meant as a parody of the horror film 'Obsession', but the context does not reach the audience. Callaway ends the partnership, Good Good steps back from a November PGA Tour title sponsorship, Golf Galaxy asks for its branding to be removed from the 'Big Break x Good Good' series, and the premiere slips from 25 August to 1 September.

Why it mattersThe gap between publishing and consequence has compressed from weeks, as in the historical cases, to hours. With no recognisable message behind the shock, the brand is left with nothing to defend itself with.
Search Engine Land, blog.google, MediaPost · Aug-Sep 2026

Google Ads: September marks the forced jump to full automation

From 1 September, campaigns using Dynamic Search Ads, auto-created assets and campaign-level broad match are auto-upgraded to AI Max, with completion expected by month end. In parallel, language targeting disappears from Search and Performance Max: the system decides on its own from creative, landing page and device language. The full sunset of Dynamic Search Ads slips to February 2027 instead.

Why it mattersThe thread we have followed since August continues. Paid loses another manual lever, and the edge moves to whoever shows up with clean data and creative ready for the system.
Industry coverage · late Aug 2026

The Trade Desk launches Kokai Zuma with the agentic assistant Koa

A demand-side platform overhaul with a redesigned interface, updated forecasting and extended agentic capability: the Koa assistant helps advertisers plan, buy, measure and optimise campaigns through conversational flows.

Why it mattersProgrammatic too is moving to a chat interface. The skill that counts shifts from knowing how to click the platform to knowing how to brief the agent well.

Publishers with an OpenAI licence get 48% more ChatGPT citations per cited page

The figure compares publishers with active licensing deals with OpenAI against publishers without a deal, on citations per page inside ChatGPT answers.

Why it mattersIt is the first numeric evidence on the thread about AI agents for shopping and search. Discoverability inside generative engines is also bought with direct deals, not only with well-structured content.

Social trends

TikTok and Reels, late Aug to early Sep (Later, NapoleonCat, Ramdam)
  • "Overwhelm reaction": audio saying "I try to act normal, stay calm, but this is exactly the scenario I feared", used over patience tests (a slow queue, someone taking forever). Hot, very specific.
  • "Netflix Documentary" chair-sit: documentary score, a chair in frame, the person walks in and sits as if for an important interview. Born on TikTok, now trending on Reels too.

Why it mattersNone of these needs budget or technical skill: shot on a phone in minutes. The barrier to entry stays very low, and that is also the theme of today's Instagram item in The world.

Campaigns & brand moves

MediaPost, WWD, PRNewswire · 4-24 Aug 2026

Heineken 0.0, 'Second Serve Tickets' with Serena Williams

Fans holding old ticket stubs from any US Open Serena Williams played could enter by sending a photo of the stub and a personal memory, to win a seat in the Heineken Suite at Arthur Ashe Stadium during the 2026 US Open. Entries closed 16 August; limited-edition cans inspired by her six US Open title ticket designs went on sale 24 August, on-tournament only.

Why it mattersThe lever is participatory nostalgia, not a passive spot. The fan becomes part of the brand's story rather than watching it, built on the stated 'Fans Have More Friends' platform.
Engage, ANSA, Borsa Italiana · on air from 3 Aug to 30 Sep 2026

Logista Italia, 'Getta Giusto'

A national campaign on correct disposal of cigarette butts, on air on TV and digital, tied to distributing pocket ashtrays through the tobacconist network Logista serves.

Why it mattersA distributor turning a regulatory and reputational problem, litter, into a branded utility handed out at the point of sale. A move worth watching for any client sitting on a logistics or retail network.

What is working in content

Vidico, Hootsuite, Later Social, Kapwing · 2026 data

Raw beats polished, with numbers

A single-take trucker lip sync did 336 million views. Most scrolling users say they prefer less-edited content, and creators respond with minimally cut talking-head. Reels now account for roughly 50% of time spent on Instagram, reaching over 2 billion monthly users.

Why it matters'Less editing' has become the expected standard on Reels and TikTok, not the exception. Production value is no longer the signal it was.

The historical bridge

1991-2000 · Ponzano Veneto

United Colors of Benetton, Oliviero Toscani

For a decade Benetton runs campaigns with no product and no copy, only charged photographs, some documentary, some staged, on race, religion, illness, death penalty. The work is banned in several countries and wins major awards. Benetton buys global fame and a clear position by refusing to advertise the sweater, until the backlash on the death-row series pushes Toscani and the brand apart in 2000.

Why it still holds. Shock without a message the audience can recognise ages badly. Toscani's images always carried an argument, uncomfortable but legible; Good Good Golf's did not, and the fallout came in hours instead of years. If a brand we work on has a known weak spot, naming it costs less than hiding it.
