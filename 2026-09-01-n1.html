It's Nice That, Dezeen, Meta · 14 Aug 2026

Instagram's first refresh in over a decade

The script wordmark was kept after a sans serif was tested. A custom handwriting face, Instagram Pen, arrives, along with Instagram Mono; Instagram Sans is widened; the sunset gradient now appears with a lighter touch rather than dominating; and a new viewfinder device derived from the camera glyph frames content across the system and draws attention back to the act of taking the picture. Team: Christy Silva and Cynthia Pratomo.

Why it mattersIt is a rebrand that deliberately gives up the brand's own spotlight. A visual-system case study for anyone working on identities where a person is the real logo (Paolo, for Verays).
TubeBuddy, creator news · from 24 Aug 2026

YouTube changes how it counts a view

A view now registers from the first frame of playback, aligning with Shorts, TikTok and Reels. In parallel, from 1 Feb 2027 the Partner Program for new applicants requires 8,000 qualified watch hours in 365 days or 20 million Shorts views in 90 days. Existing partners are unaffected.

Why it mattersFor Content-Succede, YouTube view counts before and after 24 August have to be read as two different metrics. Worth noting whenever we compare video performance.
Anicca, FlyPost · from 17 Aug, then 1 and 4 Sep 2026

Google Ads accelerates the push to automation

Target Overperformance ends, broad match and auto-generated assets auto-upgrade to AI Max from 1 September, and the Performance Max opt-out date is 4 September.

Why it mattersWhoever runs paid loses another manual lever. The competitive edge moves upstream, to clean product feeds and structured data.
Braze research, cited in roundups · Aug 2026

AI shopping agents

Projection: the share of consumers using AI shopping agents jumps from 19% to 46% by the end of 2026. These agents do not pick by brand name or ad spend, but by structured data: clean feeds, accurate pricing, clear attributes, authority signals.

Why it mattersDiscoverability is shifting from SEO to feed optimization. It applies to every client with an ecommerce.

Campaigns & brand moves

Marketing Dive · H1 2026 review

The H1 2026 thread: the best campaigns sold honesty

The common mechanism is giving up something visible to buy credibility. Burger King, 'There's A New King And It's You': mascot removed, brand decline admitted in an Oscars spot; explicit pratfall effect; Q1 2026 comparable sales +5.8%, 97% franchisee support for higher ad spend. Aerie, '100% Aerie Real' with Pamela Anderson challenging AI's ability to capture human beauty, plus a no-AI creator pledge; record Q1 revenue. Dove, 'r/eal reviews': the first 50 unfiltered Reddit reviews turned into ad creative; six Cannes Lions. Anthropic, Super Bowl spot staging AI skepticism with humour, claim 'Ads are coming to AI. But not to Claude'; Super Clio.

Why it mattersIt is field proof that 'Authenticity is the rarest luxury' is not just a tone of voice, it is a business lever. For Verays it means showing the real limits (small production, low availability, Paolo doing everything by hand) is worth more than hiding them.

What is working in content

Kapwing, Firework, various · 2026 data

Short-form retention, by the numbers

First 3 seconds decide: 65% of those who pass 3s go past 10s, and 71% decide within seconds whether to stay. Length: 21-34s for peak engagement, 50-60s for peak absolute views (avg 4.1M). Captions: +12% watch time, +80% completion; 85% of Facebook video is watched without sound. What no longer works: recognisable formula hooks, and 58% of users unfollow accounts with misleading hooks.

Why it mattersThe direction is an honest, specific hook, not a shouted one. The same principle runs through this whole issue.

The historical bridge

1962 · New York

"Avis is only No. 2 in rent a cars. So why go with us? We try harder."

Avis is second behind Hertz, holds an 11% market share and has not made a profit in 13 years. DDB, with Bill Bernbach and copywriter Paula Green, puts the brand's weakness in the headline. Within a year Avis turns a 3.2 million dollar loss into a 1.2 million profit, its first in 13 years; share goes from 11% to 29% in the first year and to about 35-36% by 1966. The campaign runs for over 50 years.

Why it still holds. Declaring your weakness before the market does defuses skepticism and buys sympathy. Burger King in 2026 runs Avis's 1962 move again. If a brand we work on has a known weak spot, naming it costs less than hiding it.
