The world

Forbes, The Creator Economy · 12 Aug 2026

Meta Partnership Ads break the 10 billion dollar run rate

The format that turns creator content into paid ads for brands hit a 10 billion dollar annualised run rate in Q1 2026, double the prior year, inside an ad stack worth around 40 billion annualised. Creator-signed ads generate 19% more clicks, 10% more conversions and cost 5% less than the same messages posted by the brand. The hub adds AI tools that pull in UGC, affiliate and brand-tagged posts automatically.

Why it mattersIt is no longer an aesthetic preference for 'authentic' content, it is a performance number with real budget behind it. The creator no longer sits next to the brand, it replaces the brand as the voice that sells better.
Meta announcement · 25 Aug 2026

Instagram opens the toolbox to creators and tightens rules for minors

First Draft auto-assembles a rough cut of selected clips, Reels get a built-in teleprompter, posts and carousels can swap soundtrack and every carousel slide can carry a different caption. In parallel, users under 16 need a parent's permission to go live or to turn off the automatic blurring of sensitive images.

Why it mattersMeta lowers the technical bar to produce publishable content again, at the same moment it certifies that this content is worth more than traditional advertising. The two announcements are the same strategy.
TechCrunch, Fortune, NBC News · 3-6 Aug 2026

OpenAI takes a group of influencers to a luxury resort, the public does not take it well

The 'Summer Camp' trip to Wildflower Farms in the Hudson Valley featured farm-to-table dinners, beekeeping, painting and sessions on OpenAI products. The creators involved took waves of criticism for promoting a company under scrutiny over environmental impact and labour conditions, just as OpenAI finalises a 500 billion dollar data centre in Ohio and a 200 million dollar contract with the Department of Defense. The official response: an 'educational' trip.

Why it mattersThe boomerang is not the luxury itself, it is the contrast between the implicit message, 'come into nature', and the company's public context. The pratfall logic runs in reverse: incoherence sinks a brand as fast as dishonesty.
Digiday podcast · 1 Sep 2026

Publishers keep losing Google traffic, the autumn opens on the same question

Publisher traffic stays compressed by Google's AI search, to the point that outlets like USA Today are weighing whether to block organic-search crawlers. The most-cited figure, a 25% drop in referral traffic from AI Overviews, comes from an August 2025 study: flagged because it keeps circulating as if it were recent.

Why it mattersThe thread we have followed since July, discoverability inside AI agents instead of classic SEO, widens from commerce to editorial content. It is no longer only 'how do I sell', but 'how do I survive if the click stops coming'.

Social trends

TikTok and Reels, early September (Later, NapoleonCat, Ramdam)
  • "I Like How I Look" (Jessie Murph audio): three-shot format, two solo shots showing confidence, then a reveal with the person, pet or passion that matters most. Climbing the audio charts.
  • "The Chair": the stronger partner lifts the other, who sits on their arms like a chair. Nascent, couples format.
  • Reality-TV audio over mundane scenes: ultra-dramatic music over a grocery run or cereal eaten in silence, for an accidental docu-drama effect. Hot.
  • Wind sound effect as a comic excuse for "accidentally" ending up somewhere you actually wanted to reach. Nascent, ironic.
Why it mattersNone of these formats needs budget or skill, shot on a phone in minutes. It confirms what earlier issues saw on raw content: the entry barrier stays very low, and that is also the theme of today's Instagram item in The world.

Campaigns & brand moves

Contagious, Campaign of the Week · late Aug 2026

Omo / Persil, 'The Book of Dirt'

Ogilvy Cape Town, with artist Karabo Poppy and printers Colourtone Aries, made a children's book that stays blank until it is touched with hands dirty with soil: only contact with dirt activates the ink and reveals the illustrations. The project extends the 'Dirt is Good' platform, live since 2018, inside the parent campaign that from 2004 to 2023 took the brand's global sales from 400 million to 4.3 billion euro.

Why it mattersThe mechanism does not illustrate the message, it forces it. You do not read the book about the benefits of outdoor play, you only activate it by playing outdoors and getting dirty. It is advertising that works as a behavioural test.
Spot and Web, Engage, Touchpoint News · on air to 6 Sep 2026

PhotoSi, 'Close your holiday in style'

A 2 million euro spend across CTV, linear TV, digital, radio and social, including Netflix and Amazon Prime Video, for the FotoLibro Travel Design Collection, over 200 covers themed on travel destinations. The message starts from a real behaviour: hours spent choosing the destination and taking photos, then it all stays locked in the phone's memory.

Why it mattersThe lever is not holiday nostalgia, it is the productive guilt of content never used. It works because it names a gesture the viewer already made last week, the camera roll of 400 photos never printed.

What is working in content

OutlierKit, industry roundups · confirmed Apr-May 2026

YouTube: satisfaction officially beats watch time

YouTube confirmed that satisfaction signals, surveys, repeat views, shares, now weigh more than raw watch time in ranking. The first 30 seconds stay the key input. Someone who watches 80% of an 8-minute video and likes it sends a stronger signal than someone who watches 40% of a 25-minute video and drops off.

Why it mattersIt changes the metric you optimise for in production. Retention is not enough any more, you need to leave the viewer satisfied they watched, and those two overlap less than they seem.
ReddiReach, industry data · 2026

Reddit as an acquisition channel, not a virality channel

Reddit passed 109 million daily users. One cited case: a SaaS campaign drove 47,128 referral visits, 312 qualified leads and a customer acquisition cost 63% lower than paid search, with 68 seconds average on page against a site average of 41. The logic is not chasing the viral post, but holding the discussions where the audience complains about a problem, answering with authority from real accounts, then turning those threads into case studies with numbers.

Why it mattersIt is the opposite of the reach-first logic of TikTok and Reels. On Reddit the return is measured in leads and citations, and it is a channel Italian brands still barely hold.

The historical bridge

1888 · Rochester, New York

"You press the button, we do the rest"

George Eastman launches the first Kodak camera with a slogan he wrote himself because he did not trust ad men. Before that, making a photograph meant knowing how to prepare plates, develop, print: a craft for a few. Eastman splits the process in two, whoever shoots and whoever develops, and for the first time anyone can produce an image knowing nothing about the technique.

Why it still holds. Eastman did not invent photography, he removed the technical skill from the path between wanting an image and having it, and so multiplied the number of people who take photos by orders of magnitude. First Draft and Instagram's teleprompter do the same to video today. The difference is what happens next: in 1888 Kodak still controlled development and therefore the business; today the platform processes data and distribution, and that is where the value moves.
