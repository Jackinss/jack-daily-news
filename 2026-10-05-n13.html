The world

Meta via IAB Global Creator Week · 16 & 29 Sep 2026

Meta's creator hub goes global, and Instagram Live becomes ad inventory

Meta launched its Creator Marketing Hub worldwide on 16 September, merging Creator Marketplace and the Partnership Ads Hub into one place to find creators, manage permissions and buy partnership ads. From 29 September, a creator's live broadcast on Instagram can be amplified as a Partnership Ad. Brands need content-level permissions with expiration dates, and ad-readiness tools can strip copyrighted music or stickers so a post qualifies. Netinfluencer's 1 October coverage cites IAB's 2025 Creator Economy Report and Meta's Q4 2025 research for the figure that creator recommendations influence 45% of consumer purchases, and for creator economy ad spend of $37 billion.

Why it mattersMeta is turning the one format creators fully control, the unscripted live stream, into paid media that brands buy through an official hub. The creator still decides what goes on air, but the audience is now a media buy.
YouTube, Made On YouTube · 23 Sep 2026

YouTube gives Shorts an AI editing partner and expands likeness protection

At its annual Made On YouTube event on 23 September, YouTube announced a "conversational AI editing partner in Shorts", better feedback inside YouTube Studio, and tools to "get noticed by brands and find resources for pitching partnerships". It also expanded likeness detection to "protect your voice and face", added real-time auto-dubbing for live streamers, and launched Live "showdowns" between creators. YouTube published no adoption or usage numbers with the announcement.

Why it mattersThe platform is building the brand-partnership layer and the protection layer at the same time. For a creator, the likeness tool answers the same problem Instagram started enforcing on 31 August, when it renamed its AI label and began cutting reach for profiles that feature an undisclosed AI-generated person.
CURE Intelligence, Google · Sep 2026

Google makes AI Max the default for eligible campaigns

Google is automatically upgrading eligible campaigns to AI Max, with campaigns using Automatically Created Assets and Broad Match affected first and DSA campaigns following in 2027. Google reports an average 7% increase in conversions. The same period brought tests of traditional search ads inside AI Mode.

Why it mattersThe 7% is Google's own average, not an independent test. The upgrade is automatic, so the only protection is to compare the upgraded campaign against its pre-upgrade numbers in your own account before accepting the default.
CURE Intelligence · 25 Sep 2026

ChatGPT ads go to 31 European countries

According to CURE Intelligence, ChatGPT ads now run in 31 European countries, including Germany and Austria, for Free and Go users. CURE gives no primary OpenAI source for the expansion, so treat the country count as a secondary report until OpenAI confirms it.

Why it mattersGoogle and OpenAI are now both selling ad inventory inside the assistant, which means the "ask an AI for a recommendation" moment is becoming a paid placement on two platforms at once.

Campaigns & brand moves

Stellantis press, Motor Online · on air from 11 Sep 2026, Italy

Citroën becomes "Sytroën" in Italy, with Omar Sy as Special Advisor

Outside the 10-day window, kept here because it is still the clearest brand mechanism on air. For the campaign, Citroën temporarily renames itself "Sytroën", and Omar Sy joins as "Special Advisor" rather than as a traditional ambassador, a role built on dialogue and ideas about mobility. Created by Marcel, directed by Hugo Gélin and produced by Soldats, it runs on TV, connected TV, radio, digital and social, with offers of the C3 from 49 euros a month and the C3 Aircross from 99 euros a month. The first film launched in France on 16 June 2026 (Stellantis press). No reach or sales results have been published.

Why it mattersThe joke is the brand's own name, so the wink costs nothing in clarity, and the price line sits directly under the pun, which keeps the offer from disappearing behind the gag.
Engage on-air list, Alkemy+ · 25 Sep to 3 Oct 2026, Italy

"Capsule Pellini. Nuova forma, stessa firma." launches a capsule format across YouTube, Mediaset, RaiPlay, Rai Digital, Sky, Prime Video, Netflix, display, social and influencer marketing. No reach, spend or sales results are published.

What is working in content

CreatorIQ report, cited by Netinfluencer · 1 Oct 2026

Creator content is 44% of paid creative on average, per the numbers Meta is quoting

Creator content makes up 44% of paid media creative on average, according to a CreatorIQ report cited by Netinfluencer. Meta's own figures, as cited there, put creator content at 20% lower cost per acquisition and 50% higher click-through rate than standard brand creative. Meta's figures are vendor claims, and the CreatorIQ number is a sample average, so both need testing on your own account.

Why it mattersThat is the number the creator market is now pricing against. If your branded spot costs more per click than a creator video with the same offer, the spot is the thing to test first.

The historical bridge

Cincinnati and New York, 1933

Ma Perkins: the brand bought the story, and the word "soap opera" followed

Radio station WLW began broadcasting "Oxydol's Own Ma Perkins" on 14 August 1933, and NBC carried it from 4 December 1933. Procter & Gamble's Oxydol soap flakes sponsored the daily serial for most of its run, and the sponsorship ended in 1956, with the show continuing until 1960 (Wikipedia, Encyclopedia.com). The show's sponsorship by a soap product is commonly cited as the origin of the term "soap opera". Wikipedia credits Ma Perkins with "giving birth to storytelling and content-based advertising".

Why it still holds. Oxydol did not interrupt the story with a spot. It bought the daily appointment and the story itself, so the audience came back for the drama and the sponsor's name rode along inside it. That is the logic of this week's Instagram Live ads and Partnership Ads: the brand pays to amplify a creator's story, not to break into the middle of it. The difference is that in 1933 the brand wrote the script, and in 2026 the creator does, which is why the money now goes through a hub instead of an agency.
